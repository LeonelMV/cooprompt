A more connected way to build

One conversation.
More momentum.

Good work happens when ideas, decisions, and execution stay connected. CooPrompt brings teammates and their AI coding agents into a shared space, so the next person can pick up with the context they need.

Built for the way teams work

Your people and agents,
on the same page.

A thoughtful toolkit for turning shared ideas into meaningful progress.

01 / COLLABORATE

Conversations in common

Bring teammates into the same chat to follow the discussion, agent activity, and decisions as they happen.

02 / CONNECT

Choose your agent

Work with Claude Code, Codex, or the Cursor CLI, and choose the model setup that fits your work.

03 / BUILD

A shared workspace

Collaborative chats can keep a working folder in sync across participants, bringing the work closer to the conversation.

04 / FOCUS

Make room for momentum

Queue messages, interrupt a turn, or work on multiple tasks in parallel when your team needs to move faster.

05 / AUTOMATE

Keep work moving

Schedule recurring agent tasks and review their results in your conversation and automation inbox.

06 / EXTEND

Connect your tools

Bring MCP connectors into your workflows, import existing configurations, or add a connector of your own.

01 / SET UP

Bring your workspace

Invite the conversation

Ask an agent, invite teammates, and follow the work as it unfolds in a shared chat.

03 / KEEP CONTEXT

Build on what happened

Shared history and workspace sync help participants pick up the thread and keep moving.
